What is Ava?

Ava is an Android app for typing with your voice and a Persian voice assistant. Speech recognition runs on the phone itself, so it works offline and your voice is never sent anywhere.

Features

🎙 Voice typing in any appTap a text field, tap the Ava orb and speak — text appears live in the field.
👑 Say “Ava”Ava answers in its own voice and runs your command, e.g. open an app or search the web. It responds only to its own name.
📱 In-app voice commandsOpen chats, write messages (never sent until you say “send”), tap buttons by voice.
✨ Smart floating buttonStays in a predictable spot above the keyboard; a golden aurora glows while you speak.
⌨️ Voice keyboardA keyboard for voice-only typing.
🔒 PrivacyOffline, no servers, no tracking, no ads. Privacy policy (Persian)
